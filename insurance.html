Quote-to-Sale Performance Dashboard

This Power BI dashboard tracks how insurance quotes convert into sold policies across agents, regions, states, and business lines. It shows a 39% quote-to-sale conversion rate, $11K in sold premiums, and a 1-day average sales cycle, then turns those metrics into findings on agent performance, pricing accuracy, and where the sales process can improve.

Project Objective

  • Measure how efficiently quotes convert into sold and active policies across agents, regions, states, and quote types.
  • Identify top-performing agents and the behaviors that drive conversions so they can be replicated across teams.
  • Evaluate pricing accuracy by comparing sold premiums to quoted premiums and surface risks to margin.

Workflow

  • Built a quote-to-policy funnel showing 49 quotes, 19 sold policies, and 15 active policies, exposing a 39% conversion rate and a drop-off after sale.
  • Tracked weekly sold premiums against conversion % by quote type (Auto vs. Business) to compare line-of-business trends over time.
  • Measured average days from quote to sale against conversion % to test whether faster closes lead to higher conversion.
  • Ranked agents by quote-to-sale rate, split by Certified and Training status, to spot top performers and high-potential trainees.
  • Compared quote volume and sold premiums across the top 3 states (TX, MS, IN) and added a forecast line to project near-term conversion %.
  • Analyzed the distribution of premium differences between quoted and sold policies to assess pricing accuracy.
  • Added slicers for Region, State, Quote Type, and Policy Status so users can filter every visual and drill into specific segments.

Results and Impact

  • Overall efficiency is strong: a 39% conversion rate, $11K in sold premiums, and a 1-day average sales cycle point to a fast, effective process.
  • Agents 8, 12, and 10 lead conversions, while Agents 15 and 18 show strong potential despite still being in training.
  • Agent 8 has higher non-pay cancellations and longer close times, suggesting gaps in payment follow-up that erode otherwise strong sales.
  • The West region outperforms the Southeast because of greater quote volume and agent coverage.
  • Commercial lines show steady growth while Auto remains volatile, signaling inconsistent pricing or customer engagement.
  • Most policies sell at or below quoted premiums, showing solid pricing accuracy, though frequent small discounts could compress margins.
  • Conversion rate shows no clear correlation with premiums or time to sell, indicating that speed and pricing consistency drive performance more than volume does.

Opportunities

  • Improve evaluation of customers' payment reliability at quote time to reduce non-pay cancellations.
  • Replicate top-agent behaviors across regions, using the West region and top-ranked agents as the benchmark.
  • Refine pricing strategy to limit small recurring discounts and support sustainable, balanced growth.

Technical Stack

  • Business Intelligence: Power BI (interactive dashboards, slicers, forecasting visuals)
  • Data Modeling: DAX measures for conversion rate, average days to sale, and premium difference
  • Data Extraction: SQL
  • Visualization: funnel, combo, donut, and histogram charts; KPI cards

Key Takeaways

  • Speed and pricing consistency matter more than raw volume, so coaching should focus on follow-through and pricing discipline.
  • Sales performance alone is not enough: payment reliability determines whether a sold policy stays active.
  • A single dashboard connecting funnel, agent, region, and pricing views turns raw quote data into clear, actionable next steps for sales leadership.
